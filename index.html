Please note: These are simple food ideas gathered from public summaries of Barbara O'Neill's talks and book, plus other published recipes. They are not medical advice. For balance: Australian health regulators barred O'Neill from providing health services in 2019 over unsubstantiated health claims. Talk to Alisa's care team before trying anything new.

High-Protein Chicken & White Bean Orzo

Chicken cutlets browned in one skillet, then sliced and served over creamy whole-wheat orzo cooked with onion, tomatoes, kale and butter beans, finished with a little cream, Pecorino Romano and fresh lemon juice. About 45 minutes, serves four.

Adapted in our own words from EatingWell, "High-Protein Chicken & White Bean Orzo." https://www.eatingwell.com/high-protein-chicken-white-bean-orzo-12064374

Marry Me Chicken & Spinach Penne Skillet

Chicken pieces cooked in a skillet with sun-dried tomatoes, shallots, garlic and Italian seasoning, then finished with wilted spinach, a little half-and-half and Parmesan, and tossed with whole-wheat penne. One pan, about 35 minutes, serves four.

Adapted in our own words from EatingWell, "Marry Me Chicken & Spinach Penne Skillet." https://www.eatingwell.com/marry-me-chicken-spinach-penne-skillet-11924756

One-Pan Chicken & Asparagus Bake

Chicken breasts baked on one sheet pan with baby potatoes and carrots, then asparagus added near the end, all finished with a lemon, shallot and mustard dressing and fresh parsley and dill. About 35 minutes, serves four.
