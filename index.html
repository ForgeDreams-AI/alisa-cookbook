Alisa's Cookbook

Simple everyday food and juice ideas, organized by meal.

Getting enough food matters most. Keeping weight steady and getting enough calories and protein comes before cutting foods out. The plant-based version of the skillet below is there because it is a preference, not a rule — any food restriction should be talked through with Alisa's care team. Nutrition numbers on this page are rough estimates, not exact values.

Please note: These are simple food ideas gathered from public summaries of Barbara O'Neill's talks and book, plus other published recipes. They are not medical advice. For balance: Australian health regulators barred O'Neill from providing health services in 2019 over unsubstantiated health claims. Talk to Alisa's care team before trying anything new.
